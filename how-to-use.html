Entering a score

  1. Tap Enter a score and choose the group you played in.
  2. Pick the four players. Start typing a name and it appears.
  3. Choose the event and enter the sets.
  4. Say who you are and tap Submit once.
  5. The match appears at the top of Recent matches straight away.
On a slow connection, give it a moment. The button locks while it saves, and if the same match has already been entered, the app tells you who entered it.

A group's page

At the top: this week's matches, who's playing, the top pair and the biggest upset, then In form. The buttons below it are Enter a score, Ratings & ladders, Group members (everyone who has played here), Court card (tonight's courts) and Tournaments. Further down: the biggest climb and steepest fall charts, recent matches and card matches. Its pills are Climb, Move, Recent, Card and Up.

My page

My page
Claim it once: tap My page, find your name and choose a 4-digit PIN. Then add a photo if you like. It shows beside your name everywhere.

You'll see your rating, level and rank, your figures, a chart of your rating since you started, and Move by group, where your rating moved in each group.

The buttons ask which group, with your own first. Tournaments lists the ones you're entered in; tap one to see its draw and standings.

The records

Your region's records have List all members (everyone, with their group), Member profiles (tap a name) and Ratings and ladders. The Global Ledger has the same for every region, plus List all clubs, showing every region and its groups.
