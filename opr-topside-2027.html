Topside

Win and you stay together. Lose and you switch sides.

A new doubles competition where the prize is a partnership. You enter on your own, the draw gives you a partner, and you keep that partner for as long as the two of you keep winning.

How a round works

1Enter on your ownOne name, no partner needed. Men and ladies are all in together, and you can join at any round.
2The draw gives you a partnerPairs are made from your OPR ratings so that every pair is as level as it can be. Everyone starts with a real chance.
3Play one match in 10 daysBest of 3 sets against the pair you are drawn to play. Arrange it when it suits the four of you, then enter the score.
You winYou stay togetherYour reign grows by one round and you defend it in the next round.
You loseYou switch sidesThe pair is split and you each get a new partner in the next draw.
The Topside champions are the pair with the longest unbroken reign: the most rounds won in a row together.
What to do from today

Your rating decides your draw. Keep it up to date.

On draw day the system reads your OPR rating as it stands. An old rating means an unfair draw, for you and for the partner you are given. So enter every match you play between now and January, social games too.

It picks your sectionThere are two sections, split by rating, so you play people around your own level.
It picks your partnerStronger players are drawn with partners who need the help, so the pairs come out level.
It is ready for what comes nextEvery future tournament seeds and draws from the same rating.
Entering a match takes half a minute: open OPR, tap Enter a score, choose your group, pick the four players and type the sets.
