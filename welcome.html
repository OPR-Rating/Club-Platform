Welcome to your club

One club. One rating. Your groups.

 

Welcome — this is your club on OPR, in one package. You're registered once, you carry one padel rating, and you can play in any of the club's groups — whichever you join, whenever you're there.

Every match you play, anywhere in the club, tells the same story.

Add it to your phone, enter your scores after each match, and watch your rating build. Your organisers handle the rest — all you do is play, have fun, and enjoy the padel.

How your club is built

The clubYour clubOpen to every member. Play here any time, with anyone — no group needed.
GroupsThe crowds you play withEach group has its own colour, its own pairs, ladder, tournaments and court cards. You join a group simply by playing a match there.
Club ledgerEveryone, one rating eachThe whole club in one list, with ladders and stats across every group.
Groups rank you. They never change your rating — that belongs to you, and it moves only when you play. The colour tells you where you are: the club is OPR purple; each group wears its own.

A night at the club

1

Someone makes the court card. In the app: ✚ Enter your match score → Create a court card. Set the event, date, match type and sets once; tick who's here (your group's regulars first, or the whole club); choose Level courts (like plays like) or Mixed courts (every court gets a strong, a middling and a weaker player); then Make the courts.

2

The app pairs everyone from their current ratings. On each court the two pairs are as evenly matched as the field allows. The card is saved straight away — every phone on the board sees it.

3

Play. Then anyone on the court taps Enter a court result, picks the court, and types the score. Names, type and sets are already filled in.

4

Ratings update on the spot. A court card match is an ordinary match — same rating, same ladder, same story. No card? Enter a score the normal way; nothing changes.

Add the app to your phone

📲 Show me how to install it ›
On iPhone (Safari): open your club → tap the Share icon ⬆️ → “Add to Home Screen”.
On Android (Chrome): open your club → tap the ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).
It then works just like an app, with its own OPR icon — one tap and you're on your club, no browser bar, no fuss.

What you can do

🎾 Inside the app ›
1

Enter your match scores — pick the four names, enter the sets. No PIN needed.

2

Follow your rating and form match by match. It's the same number wherever in the club you play.

3

See how you play with each partner — every pairing rated on its own.

4

Climb your group's ladder and issue your challenges.

5

Enter the tournaments — at your group or across the whole club — and follow the draws live.

6

Open the club ledger to see where you stand among everyone.

Your scores count the moment they're entered — the ratings update automatically.

What your club can run

🏛 Club admin — the register ›

Opens with the club's register code, from the club ledger. This is where the club itself is looked after.

1

Add members — one at a time, or paste the whole club from a spreadsheet (name, gender, starting rating).

2

Starting ratings — one default for everyone (1500 men / 1300 ladies), or a graded list from your coach.

3

Create groups — name, colour, its own code — and hand each group its link.

4

Safety net — snapshots before anything drastic, and a one-tap put-back.

A member is registered once. They appear in a group the first time they play there.
🎯 Group admin — the playing side ›

Opens with the group's own code (or the register code). Each group runs its own padel.

1

Court cards — anyone can make one; the organiser decides Level or Mixed, Matches or Pairs.

2

Tournaments — Banded Swiss, Knockout with Plate, or Round Robin; Men's, Ladies, Mixed and All Sorts divisions.

3

Handicap days — every match balanced by rating, just for fun; nobody's rating moves.

4

The ladder — challenges, positions, seasons.

5

Fix a match — correct a score or delete it; ratings recompute by themselves.

The club itself is a group too — a club-wide tournament runs from the club's own board.
