Welcome to OPR at Upper Highway Area. Everything you play here lives in one place: the groups you play in, every match you enter, a rating that follows you between groups, and a profile page that is yours alone.

Play in a group. Enter the score. Watch your page tell the story.

How it fits together

🧭 Follow the colour ›
●
A group has its own colour. Kloof Padel Ballies is blue, Sun Fun is teal. Wherever you see a group's colour — a card, a pill, a heading — you are looking at that group.
●
Purple is the region. The records of everyone at Upper Highway Area, across every group.
●
Charcoal is every registered club. The whole register, one rating each.

That is all the wayfinding you need. If the page is blue you are in Ballies; purple, the region; charcoal, everyone.

🏘️ Groups are where you play ›

Upper Highway Area is made of groups — a regular evening, a section of the club, a ladies' morning. Each group has its own board: its ladder, its court card, its tournaments, its recent matches.

Tap a group's card on the door to open it. Enter a score asks which group you played in, then takes the score. You can enter a score for any group you have played in.

🎽 Your pill, and how you get one ›

Every member carries a small coloured pill — the group they belong to. You get yours the first time you play in a group, and it stays with that group even if you play elsewhere too. If you were registered by the ledger and have not played yet, your pill reads still to play, and your page fills in with your first match.

📖 The records ›

On the door, The records has two rows. Upper Highway Area (purple) — every member here, every profile, the region's ladders. All registered clubs (charcoal) — the same three across the whole register.

Each opens on its own page: List all members (name · group · rating), Member profiles (tap a name), Ratings and ladders (pair ratings, Universal, the Club Ladder, Form). Back always returns to the page you came from.

👤 Your page ›

There is one profile page per member, and it is the same page wherever it is opened from — a records list, a partnership, a ladder. It wears your group's colour and shows your rating, level and rank; your figures; a chart of how you play; Move by group — where your rating moved and where it did not; every partnership you have ever had, grouped by group and region; and every match, with set scores under the arrow.

Claim it. From the door, tap My page, find your name, choose a 4-digit PIN. Then add a photo — it appears beside your name across the app. Only you can change it. Your name leaves the claim list once it is yours.

🔢 The rating ›

You have one rating, wherever you play in Upper Highway Area. It moves after every match, by how the result compared with what the ratings expected: beating a stronger pair moves you more than beating a weaker one. Level is your rating divided by 200. Pairs have their own rating too, which is what the ladders rank.

Your rating is shared across every region in the register, so it means the same wherever you play.

Add the app to your phone

📲 Show me how to install it ›
On iPhone (Safari): open the door → tap the Share icon ⬆️ → “Add to Home Screen”.
On Android (Chrome): open the door → tap the ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).
It then works like an app, with its own OPR icon — one tap and you are at Upper Highway Area's door.

Your first night, in five taps

🎾 Play, then enter the score ›
1
Open the door and tap Enter a score.
2
Choose the group you played in.
3
Pick the four names — start typing and your group's people appear; anyone in the register is a few letters away.
4
Enter the sets. Say who you are. Submit.
5
Tap My page. Your rating has moved, your first partnership is there, and your pill is on.
